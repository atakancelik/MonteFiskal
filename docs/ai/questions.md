# Intake questions

> Ask these before writing any code. Never ask the developer to paste a certificate, a key or a password.


The assistant asks these before writing code, in this order, and stops at the first answer that makes the plan impossible. Each question says where the developer finds the answer, so that the assistant can help instead of guessing. "Stored as" names the setting in .NET (`FiscalProfile`, `MonteFiskalOptions`, configuration section `MonteFiskal`) and in the WooCommerce plugin (option keys of `montefiskal_settings`).

## 1. Who and where

| # | Question | Where the answer comes from | Why | Stored as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which integration: a .NET application, a PHP application, or a WooCommerce shop? Which versions? | The project | MonteFiskal supports .NET 10, PHP 8.5 and the latest WordPress and WooCommerce only. Older: stop and say so. | n/a |
| 2 | Is the seller a taxpayer registered in Montenegro with a PIB? | Company registration | Without a Montenegrin PIB there is no certificate and no SEP access. Stop. | `IssuerTin` / `issuer_tin` |
| 3 | Is the seller registered for VAT? | Tax registration | Decides `IsIssuerInVAT`; a seller outside VAT sends no VAT amounts (BR-V1). | `IssuerInVat` / `issuer_in_vat` |
| 4 | Test or production now? | Developer's intent | Always start on test. Production needs the [go-live checklist](prompts/go-live.md). | `Environment` / `environment` |

## 2. Certificate

| # | Question | Where the answer comes from | Why | Stored as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Do you have a certificate for electronic signature issued to the seller's PIB by a Montenegrin certification authority (Pošta Crne Gore, CoreIT, Crnogorski Telekom)? As a file (PKCS#12) or on a token? | The certification authority | Fiscalization signs every message. A token cannot be used by a server. No certificate: only the offline tests and the fake service are possible; say so and continue with those. | `MonteFiskal:Certificate` / `MONTEFISKAL_CERT_PATH` |
| 6 | Where will the server read the certificate and its password from? | Hosting setup | Must be outside the web root and outside the repository: environment variable, secret store, or a path with restricted permissions. **Never ask the developer to paste the certificate or the password into the chat.** | `MonteFiskal:Certificate:Path`, `…:PasswordEnvironmentVariable` / `MONTEFISKAL_CERT_PATH`, `MONTEFISKAL_CERT_PASSWORD` |

## 3. Codes from the SEP portal

| # | Question | Where the answer comes from | Why | Stored as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | Business unit code (`BusinUnitCode`)? Is the unit registered for cash invoices, non-cash invoices or both? Type "internet trgovina"? | SEP, My data, Business units | Ten characters, generated by the portal. The cash or non-cash registration decides the deposit duty (BR-D5). | `BusinessUnit` / `business_unit`; cash unit: `AutoInitialDeposit` / `auto_initial_deposit` |
| 8 | Operator code (`OperatorCode`)? | SEP, My data, Operators (generated after entering the operator's personal number) | Required on every invoice. For a web shop it is the person responsible for the shop. | `Operator` / `operator` |
| 9 | Is the software registered on the SEP, and what is its code (`SoftCode`)? Who is registered as maintainer (`MaintainerCode`)? | SEP, Software | A company that uses the software and owns its installation may register it itself (BR-R7). If not registered yet, point to the setup guide's step and continue on test. | `Software`, `Maintainer` / `software`, `maintainer` |
| 10 | Is a TCR (ENU) already registered for this shop or cash register, and what is its code (`TCRCode`)? If not, should MonteFiskal register it through the service? | SEP, Business units, ENU; or the response of `RegisterTcr` | Every invoice needs a TCR code, non-cash ones included (BR-K17). Registration needs questions 7 to 9 first. | `Tcr` / `tcr`, `tcr_internal_id` |

## 4. What is sold and how it is paid

| # | Question | Where the answer comes from | Why | Stored as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Which payment methods does the shop accept: card online, bank transfer, cash on delivery, vouchers, other? | The shop | Maps to `CARD`, `ACCOUNT`, `BANKNOTE`, … and through them to a cash or non-cash invoice (BR-K3). A consumer card payment is a **cash** invoice. | Your mapping to `PaymentMethod` / `payment_map` |
| 12 | Do companies buy from you, and do you collect their tax number at checkout? | The shop | A buyer with a tax number gets a `Buyer` element. Whether a card is a company card cannot be seen from the gateway; default is `CARD` (Q-29). | `InvoiceDraft.Buyer` / buyer tax number field, `business_card` |
| 13 | When is the order paid relative to shipment: at checkout, on delivery, by invoice later? Do you take pre-orders or deposits? | The shop | Payment before shipment is formally an advance (Q-24). Default: one invoice at the moment payment is confirmed. Pre-orders and deposits: use the advance flow. The developer's accountant decides; the assistant states the two options and does not choose silently. | Your code / `mode` (`single` or `advance`) |
| 14 | Which VAT rates apply to your products (21 %, 15 %, 7 %, 0 %), and is anything exempt? Under which article? | The seller's accountant | Rates come from the shop's tax settings; an exempt line needs an exemption code (BR-V5). The assistant does not decide tax treatment. | `InvoiceLine.VatRate`, `InvoiceLine.Exemption` / `exemption_map` |
| 15 | Are prices entered with VAT included? | The shop | Selects the gross or the net calculation path (BR-A5 to BR-A7). | `InvoiceLine.PriceIncludesVat` / WooCommerce tax setting |
| 16 | Do you sell in a currency other than EUR? | The shop | Needs the currency block and an exchange rate (BR-K12). | `InvoiceDraft.Currency` |
| 17 | How does the buyer receive the invoice: paper in the parcel, e-mail, both? Do you sell digital goods? | The shop | A cash invoice must be handed over on paper (BR-P4). Digital goods: not officially settled (Q-28); say so. | `MonteFiskal.Rendering` / `print_mode`, e-mail block |
| 18 | Do you refund partially? | The shop | Decides whether partial corrective invoices are needed (BR-C5). | n/a |

## 5. Operations

| # | Question | Where the answer comes from | Why | Stored as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 19 | Where are invoices and sequence numbers stored, and is that store backed up? | The application | The ordinal must be gap-free per TCR and year and survive restarts (BR-N5); fiscal records are kept for years (BR-X1). | `EfOutboxStore`, `PdoOutboxStore` / plugin tables |
| 20 | Does anything else issue fiscal invoices for the same TCR (a cash register, an ERP)? | The seller | Two issuers on one TCR break the sequence. Use a separate TCR for the shop. | n/a |
| 21 | Who is told when an invoice could not be sent within the deadline? | The seller | Unsent invoices must be delivered within 48 hours (BR-O1, BR-O12); someone must see the alert. | `DeliveryDeadline` / `deadline_warning_hours` |

Stop conditions the assistant must state plainly: no Montenegrin PIB; runtime older than supported; certificate only on a token for an unattended server; the developer wants to skip the test environment; the developer asks to reuse a certificate found in another repository.
